🔒 100% OFFLINE DATA PRIVACY & BACKUPS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AIR-GAPPED SECURITY:
   All your gemstone inventory, purchase costs, valuations, sales invoices, client
   ledgers, and brokerage deals are stored 100% locally on your computer. Nothing is ever
   uploaded to external servers or third parties.

2. AUTOMATIC WEEKLY BACKUPS:
   Shreeji Gems automatically generates a safe database snapshot every 7 days.

3. MANUAL 1-CLICK BACKUPS ("💾 Backup Now"):
   Click the "Backup Now" button in the top navigation bar anytime to export and download
   a timestamped .json database backup file to your computer or USB drive.

4. ONE-CLICK RESTORE ("🔄 Restore"):
   If you ever switch computers or want to roll back, click "Restore" at the top to load
   any saved backup snapshot instantly.

5. EXECUTIVE BUSINESS VALUATION & P&L REPORT ("📊 Business Report"):
   Click "Business Report" at the top to preview and print a comprehensive breakdown
   of your vault valuation, total cost vs asking price, and itemized gemstone inventory.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🏷️ QR CODE TAGS & PHYSICAL BARCODE SCANNER INTEGRATION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PACKET QR LABELS:
   Every intake gemstone lot/packet generates printable QR codes with complete stone
   specifications (Variety, Shape, Carat Weight, Pieces, MM Size, Lab Certificate, etc.).

2. PHYSICAL SCANNER GUN READY:
   Plug any handheld USB or wireless barcode/QR scanner gun into your computer.
   Whenever you scan a physical packet tag, Shreeji Gems instantly detects the scan
   and automatically pops open that packet's complete dossier on screen!


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📁 HOW TO TRANSFER TO ANOTHER COMPUTER / USB DRIVE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can copy this entire folder ("Shreeji_Gems_Client_Delivery") onto any USB pendrive
and paste it onto any other Windows laptop or PC.
Run "Create_Desktop_Shortcut.bat" on the new PC, and you are ready to work!
